## Common Drywall Repair Problems in Paramus Homes

Paramus is a mix of mid-century ranches, split-levels, and newer construction — and each type of home tends to have its own set of wall and ceiling issues. Here's what we run into most often:

### Nail Pops and Stress Cracks

This is probably the most common issue we see in Paramus homes, especially in houses built in the 1960s and 70s. As the wood framing behind your drywall expands and contracts with seasonal temperature changes, it pushes nails back through the surface. You'll notice small bumps or circular cracks on the wall. They're usually cosmetic, but they can spread if ignored. The fix involves resetting the fastener, applying joint compound, and properly feathering and blending the repair so it's invisible.

### Water Damage from Roof Leaks and Plumbing

Bergen County gets plenty of precipitation throughout the year, and Paramus is no exception. Roof leaks are a leading cause of ceiling damage, and even a small plumbing drip inside a wall can saturate drywall quickly. Water-damaged drywall needs to be removed and replaced — you can't just paint over it. If you notice discoloration, sagging, or a soft spot on a wall or ceiling, get it looked at before mold becomes part of the conversation. Our [ceiling repair services](/services/ceiling-repair/) are specifically designed to handle these situations properly.

### Holes from Hardware, Door Handles, and Renovations

Whether you're rearranging furniture, removing an old TV mount, or the kids got a little too enthusiastic with a doorknob, holes in drywall are incredibly common. Small holes (under an inch or so) can sometimes be handled with a patch kit, but anything larger really benefits from professional repair — especially if you want it to actually be invisible after painting. Matching the texture of the surrounding wall is the hard part, and it's where DIY repairs often fall short.

### Cracks from Foundation Settling

Some Paramus homes — particularly older ones in established neighborhoods — show diagonal cracks running from the corners of windows and door openings. These often indicate foundation settling, which is normal in many cases but worth monitoring. Once the structural concern is addressed (or ruled out by a professional), the cosmetic repair involves cutting back the crack, applying mesh tape, and building up layers of compound. We always recommend confirming there's no active structural movement before doing final repairs.

### How long does a typical drywall repair take in Paramus?

Most repairs are completed in a single visit. Larger jobs — like water damage affecting multiple sections of drywall — may require two visits to allow compound to dry properly before final sanding and finishing.

In most cases, yes. After patching and finishing, the repaired area will need to be primed and painted to fully blend with the surrounding wall. We offer [painting services](/services/painting/) as an add-on, so you can have everything done in one visit.

### Can you match the texture on my existing walls?

Absolutely. Texture matching is one of the things we get asked about most — and it's one of our specialties. ### Should I repair or replace water-damaged drywall?

If the drywall has been wet long enough to become soft, crumbly, or develop mold, it needs to be replaced — not just patched. We'll assess the damage honestly and let you know exactly what's needed.
