## Common Drywall Repair Problems in Bergenfield Homes

### Cracks Around Doors, Windows, and Corners

This is probably the most common complaint we hear from Bergenfield homeowners. Many of the homes here were built in the 1950s through the 1970s, and the wood framing has had decades to shift and settle. That natural movement shows up as diagonal cracks radiating from the corners of door and window frames, or as hairline fractures along taped seams. Most of these are cosmetic — the result of seasonal expansion and contraction — but some indicate settling that's worth keeping an eye on.

### Water Damage and Staining

Bergen County's older housing stock often comes with plumbing that's seen better days, and even a slow drip from an upstairs bathroom or a roof that needs attention can leave its mark on your drywall. Water-damaged drywall can look like discoloration, bubbling paint, or soft, crumbling sections of wall or ceiling. The tricky part is that you can't just slap paint over it — the source needs to be fixed first, and then the damaged drywall should be properly replaced and primed before finishing. Skipping steps here almost always means the stain comes back.

### Holes from Everyday Life

Door handles punching through walls, anchors that got yanked out wrong, shelving that came down and took a chunk of drywall with it — these are the bread-and-butter repairs we handle constantly. Bergenfield homes with kids, active households, or recent furniture rearrangements are especially prone to this kind of damage. The good news: holes of almost any size can be patched cleanly with the right technique and materials, leaving walls that look like nothing ever happened.

### Nail Pops and Tape Seams

Older homes in particular tend to develop nail pops — those little bumps where a drywall nail has worked its way out from the framing behind it. You'll also see failed tape seams where the joint compound has cracked or separated over the years. Neither of these is structurally serious, but they're visually distracting and tend to get worse if ignored. Both are very repairable with the right prep work.

Pricing varies based on the size and complexity of the repair, but here are some general ranges to give you a realistic starting point:

- **Small holes and dents** (under 6 inches): typically **$75–$150** per patch

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can you match the texture on my walls if it's different from standard smooth drywall?**

Yes — texture matching is one of our core services. Bergenfield homes often have orange peel, knockdown, or other textured finishes, and blending a repair into that texture seamlessly takes skill and experience. We'll assess the existing texture before we start and replicate it as closely as possible so the repair isn't visible once it's painted.

**Do I need to fix the source of a leak before you repair the drywall?**

Absolutely, yes. If there's an active or unresolved leak, we'll tell you upfront that the drywall repair needs to happen after the water source is addressed. Repairing drywall over an ongoing moisture problem is a waste of time and money — the damage will return. Once you've had a plumber or roofer confirm the issue is resolved, we're ready to come in and restore the walls.

**How long does a typical drywall repair take?**

Most standard repairs — a few holes, some cracks, a patch job — can be completed in a few hours to a half day. Larger repairs or those requiring multiple coats of joint compound (which needs drying time between coats) may require a follow-up visit. We'll give you a realistic timeline when we come out for the estimate.

**Will I need to repaint after the repair?**

In most cases, yes. Even the most seamless patch needs to be primed and painted to blend with the surrounding wall. We offer [painting services](/services/painting/) as well, so if you'd like us to handle the full job from repair through final paint, we can do that. Otherwise, we'll leave you with a paint-ready surface for your own painter or a DIY touch-up.
