## Common Drywall Repair Problems in Hackensack Homes

### Cracks from Seasonal Temperature Changes

Hackensack's climate puts significant stress on building materials. The temperature swings from our humid summers to cold winters cause your home's framing to expand and contract. This movement often creates hairline cracks along seams and corners, particularly noticeable in homes built in the 1960s-80s throughout the city's residential areas.

### Water Damage from Roof Leaks and Plumbing Issues

With Hackensack's proximity to the Hackensack River and our area's occasional heavy rainfall, water damage is a common concern. We frequently see water-damaged drywall in homes near the river valley, where groundwater levels can fluctuate. Older homes in the downtown area often experience plumbing-related water damage in bathrooms and kitchens.

### Nail Pops and Screw Pops

The settling that's common in Bergen County's clay soil conditions often causes nail pops and screw pops in drywall. This is especially prevalent in split-level homes popular in Hackensack's suburban neighborhoods. These small circular bulges or cracks appear when fasteners work loose from the framing.

### Impact Damage and Holes

From moving furniture up narrow staircases in colonial-style homes to everyday accidents with kids and pets, holes in drywall happen to every homeowner. Whether it's a doorknob that went through the wall or damage from a home renovation project, these repairs need professional attention to look seamless.
