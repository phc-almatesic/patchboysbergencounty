## Common Drywall Repair Problems in Garfield Homes

Garfield's housing stock presents some interesting challenges when it comes to wall maintenance. Here are the most frequent issues we see in local homes:

### Settlement Cracks from Older Foundations

Many homes in Garfield were built between the 1940s and 1970s, and even well-maintained foundations can experience minor settling over decades. This often shows up as hairline cracks where walls meet ceilings, or small diagonal cracks near door frames and windows. While usually cosmetic, these cracks can worsen over time if not properly addressed.

### Nail Pops from Temperature Changes

Bergen County's temperature swings—from summer heat to freezing winters—cause building materials to expand and contract. This movement can push drywall nails or screws slightly outward, creating small bumps or actual nail heads poking through the wall surface. This is particularly common in homes with forced-air heating systems.

### Impact Damage from Daily Life

Active families inevitably deal with holes from doorknobs hitting walls, furniture bumps, or the occasional sports ball incident. Kids' rooms, hallways, and areas near stairs see the most action. While small holes might seem like DIY projects, proper repair ensures they won't show through paint or reappear later.

### Water Damage from Roof or Plumbing Issues

Garfield's mature trees are beautiful, but falling branches during storms can sometimes damage roofs. Additionally, older plumbing in vintage homes occasionally leads to minor leaks that can soften or stain drywall. Water-damaged drywall needs complete replacement to prevent mold and ensure structural integrity.
